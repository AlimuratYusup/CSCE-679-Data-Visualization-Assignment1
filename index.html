Hong Kong Temperature Heatmap

A Year × Month matrix of Hong Kong temperatures (1997 – 2017), built with D3.js. Choose a level below.

Level 1

Year / Month Heatmap

Monthly maximum or minimum temperature for every month, 1997 – 2017. Click to switch, hover for values.

Level 2

Heatmap + Daily Line Charts

Last 10 years, with a mini line chart of daily max and min temperature inside every cell.
Run locally with python -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000.
